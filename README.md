# Personal University Notes

This repository contains my personal notes taken during my time at university, spanning various courses and subjects. They were created primarily for my own study and revision purposes.

## About These Notes

These notes are tailored to my specific learning needs, the particular courses I took, and the way my professors chose to present the material. They reflect my personal understanding -- and, at times, my personal confusion -- of the topics covered. What is emphasized here may differ significantly from what your own curriculum prioritizes.

The notes are written and structured for use with ObsidianMD. Internal links, callouts, tags, and other formatting conventions follow Obsidian's syntax and may not render correctly in other Markdown editors or directly on GitHub.
